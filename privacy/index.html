Rivo privacy policy.

Updated November 11, 2026

What Rivo is

  • Rivo is a social app for music: you rate albums and songs, log the shows and festivals you go to, keep memories, and talk about music with people you follow.

What you give us

  • Your account: your email address (or the Google account you sign in with), a password if you use one, your display name, username, bio, and profile picture.
  • What you post: ratings, reviews, show and festival logs with your takes, plans, memories, lists, comments, likes, your Radar, top 5 picks, and photos.
  • Who you follow, who follows you, the people you tag at shows, and the people you block.
  • Your phone number, only if you add it in Settings so friends can find you. We keep a scrambled (salted and hashed) version and the last two digits, never the number itself.
  • Reports you send about posts or people.

What we don't collect

  • No ads, no ad tracking, and no selling or renting your information to anyone.
  • No location tracking. Venues are what you choose to log.
  • Contacts never leave your phone: Find friends compares scrambled versions of the numbers on your phone and keeps none of them.

Who sees what

  • Everything is public by default to people signed in to Rivo. Anything can be kept private: any log, list, plan, rating, or your whole profile. Private things are seen only by you; a private profile is seen only by people you approve.
  • Photos follow the privacy of what they're on.
  • If you block someone, neither of you sees the other's profile or activity.
  • Moderators can see reported posts in order to review them.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, posts, and photos.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, and Setlist.fm provide music details, album covers, and setlists. Searches you type are sent to them, without your name or account.
  • Notifications on your lock screen are scheduled on your phone.

Keeping and deleting

  • We keep your information while you have an account.
  • You can delete any post at any time, and delete your whole account in Settings > Delete account. That removes your profile, posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, and phone number right away. Backups are cleared within 30 days.

Children

  • Rivo isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

  • If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app for anything important.
  • Questions or requests: letsrivo@gmail.com.
